Privacy

samfran · samfran.rudnam.com · updated November 16, 2026

samfran is an app for two people: the restaurants they go to and one person's work shifts. What a pair keeps in it is seen only by the two of them. Nothing in it is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train anything.

What samfran keeps

  • Your email address and, if you use one, a hash of your password. If you sign in with Google, samfran receives your email address and Google's ID for your account.
  • What the two of you add: restaurants, visits, ratings and notes, and shifts (a code per day, such as 13F, and on working days the names of coworkers on the same shift, as printed on the sheet).

Photos of shift sheets

A photo of a shift sheet is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to read one person's row, and the names of the others on the same shift each working day. The photo is never stored, and nothing else on the sheet is kept. When Claude reads it through samfran's connector instead, that happens in your Claude account, under Anthropic's terms.

Where it's kept, and deleting it

samfran runs on Amazon Web Services in Sydney (ap-southeast-2), with nightly backups kept for 30 days. Deleting your account deletes your data; what you share with the other person stays theirs until they delete theirs.
